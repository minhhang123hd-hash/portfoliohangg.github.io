About Me

Đoàn Thị Minh Hằng

I'm a third-year International Business student at the Foreign Trade University, with a deep love for communications and storytelling. I've served as Vice Head of the Communications Board of the Student Union, and as MultiMedia Lead for TEDxFTUHanoi 2025 — where I got to build content and connect with so many bright young minds.

Emotionally, I lean introverted — I observe quietly, feel deeply, and choose to express myself through what I create. But that never makes me hesitant. I actively step out of my comfort zone, chase new challenges, and I'm never afraid to begin something I've never done before.

I believe creativity doesn't come from being the loudest in the room — it comes from truly understanding yourself and growing a little every day. That belief is the compass guiding me, step by step, toward shining in my own way.
